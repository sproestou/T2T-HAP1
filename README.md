# T2T-HAP1
The telomere-to-telomere genome assembly for HAP1 human parental cell line, a near-haploid cell line derived from the KBM-7 chronic myelogenous leukemia (CML) cells. 

We generated 29.1× PacBio HiFi and 52.3× ONT sequencing data. Then, we constructed a telomere-to-telomere assembly using [Hifiasm v0.25.0](https://github.com/chhylp123/hifiasm). Short-read 37x Illumina WGS data from [Jado, J.C. et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-63943-7) SRA (SRR10990525) was used for quality control. All resources are available on this site.

## Genome Quality Control

### Accuracy
The genome is highly accurate across multiple error detection tools: [merqury](https://github.com/marbl/merqury), [hmm-flagger](https://github.com/marbl/merqury), and [NucFlag (v1.0.0)](https://github.com/logsdon-lab/NucFlag). 

Merqury QV: 52.6961 (99.9995% accurate)

HMM-Flagger (HiFi): 98.88%
HMM-Flagger (ONT): 99.59%

NucFlag QV: 71.71 (99.999993% accurate)

### Contiguity
The raw genome assembly is 3.08Gb total with a contig N50 of 145.48Mb, after manual scaffolding based on alignments to the T2T-CHM13v2.0 assembly, contig N50 rose to 158.64 Mbp. There are 12 T2T chromosomes in the currently HAP1v1.0 assembly: 1, 3, 8, 9_22, 10, 11, 17, 18, 19, 22_9, and X. 

[QuarTeT](https://github.com/aaranyue/quarTeT) was used to evaluate the telomere-to-telomere quality of the assembled genomes across contigs. 

Both telomere found: 12
Only one telomere found: 15
No telomere found: 263
